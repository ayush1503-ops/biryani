bir·ya·ni

/bɪrˈjɑː.ni/ noun, Urdu

A pot of rice that has been lied to about how long it takes. Layered with marinated meat, saffron milk and onions fried until they stopped arguing, sealed with dough, then left completely alone over a low flame until the whole gali can smell it. Eaten with the hands, argued over, followed by a nap.

Synonyms dawatkhushiyanbohot zyada

Not a pulao. Don't do that. We will know.

def. 01 · opposite of: biryani in 20 minutes

02Dum pukht · the method

Ek deg,
itna simple.

Five things happen. None of them fast. Scroll karo, dikhata hoon.

01/ 05

  1. 01 Chawal Basmati, soaked 40 minutes. Not 20. Layered half-cooked, never fully.
  2. 02 Marinade Dahi, raw papaya, Kashmiri mirch, a spoon of mustard oil. Overnight.
  3. 03 Birista Onions, three hours, low flame, no shortcuts, no crying in the deg.
  4. 04 Tarkib Rice over meat. Saffron milk, mint, kewra, ghee. Then the same again.
  5. 05 Dum Atta seal. 25 minutes on low, no peeking. Then opened in front of you.
