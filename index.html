Bir·ya·ni

/bɪrˈjɑː.ni/ · noun, Urdu

A pot of rice that has been lied to about how long it takes. Layered with marinated meat, saffron milk and onions fried until they stopped arguing, sealed with dough, then left completely alone over a low flame until the whole gali can smell it.

itna simple.

Five things happen. None of them fast.

Step 01 / 05

01

Chawal

Basmati, soaked 40 minutes. Not 20. Layered half-cooked, never fully.

02

Marinade

Dahi, raw papaya, Kashmiri mirch, a spoon of mustard oil. Overnight.

03

Birista

Onions, three hours, low flame, no shortcuts.

04

Tarkib

Rice over meat. Saffron milk, mint, kewra, ghee. Then the same again.

05

Dum

Atta seal. 25 minutes on low, no peeking. Then opened in front of you.
